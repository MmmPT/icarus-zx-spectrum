# ICARUS v1.2

A major update to ICARUS for the ZX Spectrum 48K and 128K.

## Highlights

- New loading screen artwork by **Mário Viegas**.
- New 48×40 portraits for Zeus, Cronos and Medusa.
- New AY music: main theme, boss themes, calm title version and game-over elegy.
- Animated wings, shield/stone states, parallax stars, cloud band and rising sun.
- Continued play beyond level 7 with score multipliers, tougher bosses and melting wax.
- Improved Kempston joystick support throughout the game and menus.
- Numerous gameplay, sound, UI and stability fixes.

The original **v1.0 remains available** and has not been replaced.

See `CHANGELOG.md` for the full list of changes.
